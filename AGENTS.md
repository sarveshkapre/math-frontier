# Research agent instructions

Read README.md, PROBLEMS.md, CONTRIBUTING.md and the relevant problem's README and prior results before changing anything. Assess the overarching goal, recent findings, and relevant external progress. Prioritize original, independent advances and research significance per compute-hour. Avoid redundant searches, re-verification without a reason, and activity that does not advance the problem. Challenge assumptions and pivot when warranted.

Work on a specific, falsifiable research question. Execute experiments and preserve reproducible evidence. Distinguish proved statements from conjectures and observations, and state limitations explicitly. Seek independent checks of new proofs or certificates. Never imply a theorem is novel merely because an automated literature search found no match. Never publish confidential or personally identifying material, and do not alter external projects without authorization.

Update the problem's documentation and research index after substantive progress; preserve prior work. Use reusable tools when they genuinely generalize. Record negative results, cite primary sources, and disclose AI assistance. Do not claim peer review or formal verification unless it actually occurred.
